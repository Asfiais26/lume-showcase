Lume: A Neuroinclusive Developmental Portal

> A data-driven digital platform designed to support children with ADHD through gamified cognitive play while providing real-time analytical insights for parents and medical professionals.

---

1. **Features**

  A) **Gamified Cognitive Play (For Children)**
- Interactive, low-friction games specifically designed to engage children with ADHD.
- Focuses on improving attention span, working memory, impulse control, and sensory regulation.


B) **Performance Analytics & Graphs (For Parents & Doctors)**
- Real-time data logging tracks game interaction, reaction time, and play frequency.
- Automated generation of visual graphs highlighting behavioral and cognitive metrics.
- Exportable metrics for pediatricians, therapists, and parents to evaluate developmental progress over time.

---

2. **Tech Stack**

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript *
- **Backend:** Java *
- **Data Visualization:** Python *
- **Database:** MongoDB *

---

3. **Impact & Purpose**
   
Lume bridges the gap between technology and behavioral psychology. By gamifying developmental exercises, it removes the stress of routine assessments for children while delivering objective, quantitative data to care providers to tailor support strategies.
